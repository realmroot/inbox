# Governance

Realmroot Inbox is maintained by the Realmroot organization under a
maintainer-led, review-based model.

Protocol and security decisions require:

- a public design issue;
- evidence from applicable standards and provider contracts;
- an explicit compatibility and migration assessment;
- maintainer approval before implementation;
- independent outcome and engineering review for non-mechanical changes.

The project favors interoperable standards over Realmroot-specific extensions.
When an extension is unavoidable, its scope, versioning, conformance tests, and
retirement conditions must be documented.

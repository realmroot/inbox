# Code of Conduct

We are committed to a welcoming, respectful, and harassment-free community.

Participants should:

- be constructive and assume good faith;
- critique ideas and evidence, not people;
- respect privacy and never publish private messages or credentials;
- avoid harassment, discrimination, threats, and disruptive behavior;
- accept maintainer decisions about scope, safety, and compatibility.

Maintainers may edit or remove contributions and restrict participation when
behavior threatens people, the community, or the project. Report conduct issues
privately to the Realmroot organization maintainers.

This policy is based on the principles of the Contributor Covenant 2.1.
